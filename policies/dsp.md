# DSP Accommodations

This page has information for students who are enrolled in (or plan to enroll in) the Disabled Students' Program (DSP). For more information about DSP, see <https://dsp.berkeley.edu/>.

## Exams

### Exam Logistics Form

We will release an exam logistics form on Ed a few weeks before each exam.

If you need any alterations on the exam (e.g. alternate time, online exam, additional DSP accommodations not on your letter, DSP Proctoring Center, etc.), please fill out this form to let us know.

Other requests will get lost and won't be considered. Do not email instructors or individual TAs with these requests, because they will be lost. Please send everything through the form instead.

If you do not fill out the form, we will default you to the regular exam time (see below), in-person, proctored by TAs, with the accommodations on your DSP letter.


### Exam Proctoring

We will be proctoring DSP exams ourselves, proctored by TAs, in a way that meets your accommodations.

We usually proctor exams in the classrooms on the ground floor of Evans Hall ([see here for a description of the classrooms](https://rtl.berkeley.edu/classroom-database/evans-0051)), or in meeting rooms and offices in Soda Hall. If you have reduced-distraction accommodations, there will be no more than 6-7 students per classroom.

**You do not need to request proctoring from the DSP Proctoring Center**, except in two cases:
1. If you prefer to use the DSP Proctoring Center, please fill out the exam logistics form (released a few weeks before each exam). Use the box at the end of the form to explicitly say that you want to be proctored by the DSP Proctoring Center.

    If you do not fill out the form, or fill out the form late, we will default you to being proctored by TAs.
2. If we are unable to meet your accommodations ourselves, we will send you an email before each exam, asking you to request proctoring through the DSP Proctoring Center.

There are no other exam times besides the ones listed above. There are no remote exams at alternate times.

We are only offering the alternate exam time if you are unable to take the exam at the normal time. For example, if you have another exam at the same time, you can take the alternate-time exam. However, wanting a break between non-conflicting exams would not be a valid reason to take the alternate-time exam, because in this case you are able to take the exam at the normal time.

We will release an exam logistics form before each exam. To request the online or alternate-time exam, you will need to fill out this form. If you don't fill out the form, we will default you to the first option (in-person, not the alternate time).

### Pending Accommodations

This section is for students who are in the process of registering with DSP, but will not officially receive their letter of accommodation until after the exam date.

If this applies to you, then we can offer you an _exam consideration_:

**Before the exam**: Fill out the exam logistics form (released a few weeks before each exam), and indicate the accommodations that you are requesting. In the text box at the end of the form, write something like "I am requesting an exam consideration."

**After the exam**: As soon as you receive your DSP letter of accommodation, send the letter to {{ site.staff_email }}. We know the DSP office can be slow, so it is okay if you receive your DSP letter a long time after the exam, or even after the end of the semester (e.g. in the case of a consideration on the final exam).

If the accommodations you requested are on your DSP letter, then you are good to go.

If the accommodations you requested do not match your DSP letter, we will work with you to figure out next steps, based on how big the discrepancy is. While we hope to avoid it, we do reserve the right to void your exam score and have you make up the exam score some other way (e.g. clobbering a voided midterm with your final exam z-score, or receiving an incomplete and taking next semester's exam).


## Extensions

### Requesting Extensions

If you need extra time on any assignment, please fill out [the extensions form]({{ site.aux_links["Extensions"] }}).

Other requests will get lost and won't be considered. Do not email instructors or individual TAs with extension requests, because they will be lost. Please send everything through the form instead.


### Processing Extensions

We will process your requests in accordance with your accommodations; for example, if you have the "Extensions of up to 3 days" accommodation, we will approve extension requests up to 3 days for you.

Note that we do not automatically extend deadlines, even if you have extensions accommodations on your letter. You will still need to fill out the form to request the extensions.

If you need a longer extension than what's listed on your letter, you can still fill out the form, and we will process your request on a case-by-case basis.

If you don't have extensions accommodations on your letter, you can still fill out the form, and we will process your request on a case-by-case basis.

## Data Access

Your DSP information will be kept confidential between the staff tagged with "DSP Data Access" on our [staff page](/staff).

If you have any questions about accommodations, please email {{ site.staff_email }} (not individual instructors or TAs). Only the TAs with the "Staff Email Access" tag on the [staff page](/staff) will read these emails.

After the end of the semester, upon request, we can also delete any emails that mention your DSP accommodations.
