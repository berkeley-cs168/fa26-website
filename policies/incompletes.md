# Incomplete Grades

[Incomplete Request Form](https://forms.gle/bk2wPbt29cw6BWAo9)

## What is an incomplete grade?

Refer to these links for information regarding incomplete grades:
- [Office of the Registrar page](https://registrar.berkeley.edu/faculty-staff/grading/incomplete-grades/)
- [L&S advising page](https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/incomplete-grades)
- [CoE page](https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/policies-procedures/grades/)
- [CDSS page](https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/policies/registration-enrollment#incomplete-grades)

If you request an incomplete grade, you get a temporary grade of "I" on your transcript, and you get some extra time after the end of the semester to complete the last few assignments. Once you have finished the remaining assignments, or the extended deadline has passed, we will replace the "I" grade with your actual grade.


## What are the assignments I can extend as part of my incomplete?

When you request an incomplete, you need to list out all the assignments that you intend to extend past the end of the semester. Any assignments that you don't list will still be due at the end of the semester.

You can extend exactly one of these assignments as part of your incomplete:
- One exam (midterm or final exam). You cannot take an exam twice, i.e. you can't take two final exams or two midterms.
- One project

If you would like to work on other assignments as part of your incomplete (e.g. other projects), you're welcome to submit them, but we cannot guarantee that they will be included in your final grade.


## What are the deadlines to complete the assignments?

The deadlines for completing assignments are as follows:

- The exam must be taken in **{{ site.next_semester_full }}**.
    - See this link for exam times: <{{ site.url }}/fa26-faqs/>
    - There are no guarantees of alternate exam times or remote exams, so if you want to take an exam in summer, you must be available, in-person, at the listed exam time.
- Other assignments included in your incomplete must be completed by **October 1, 2026**, 11:59PM PT.
- All assignments not included in your incomplete must be completed by **August 14, 2026**, 11:59PM PT.

If you do not complete an assignment by its deadline, we will assign a score of 0 on that assignment when computing your final grade.

Note: The deadlines we use are not the default deadlines given by the university, so please ignore any due dates/lapse dates you see on CalCentral. We will assign grades according to the schedule above.


## How do I complete my assignments?

For homework, labs, and projects, please complete them in the **{{ site.semester_full }}** Gradescope.

For exams, we will reach out with exam logistics in **{{ site.next_semester_full }}**. There will be a form that you can fill out to request DSP accommodations, alternate exam times (if they are offered, no promises), and/or an online exam (if they are offered, no promises).


## How will my grade be calculated?

All grading policies from {{ site.semester_full }}, including point breakdowns and grading bins, will still apply.

If your incomplete includes an exam, we will use z-scores to scale your exam score from {{ site.next_semester_full }} to {{ site.semester_full }}.

If you skipped the midterm (replacing it with your final exam z-score), and you also take an incomplete on the final exam, then you will take only the final exam in {{ site.next_semester_full }}. Your z-score on the {{ site.next_semester_full }} final exam will be scaled to calculate scores for both your {{ site.semester_full }} midterm and final exam.

When we resolve your incomplete, we will also send you a grade report with a breakdown of your scores, and you can let us know if you spot any mistakes.


## What are the drawbacks of an incomplete grade?

Students resolving an incomplete grade have a few drawbacks:
- Students resolving an incomplete grade will have extremely limited staff support, and staff will prioritize students currently enrolled in the course.
- Students resolving an incomplete grade may not be enrolled in the class while resolving the incomplete grade, which means that you have to maintain a full course load while resolving the incomplete grade.
- We will not be able to resolve your incomplete and assign you a grade right away. For example, if you take the final exam in August, we will likely not be able to assign you a grade until September, after we finish computing grades for current students.
- Students with too many unfinished incompletes may be blocked from registration or have other adverse impacts. See the [L&S website](https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/incomplete-grades), the [CoE website](https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/policies-procedures/grades/), or the [CDSS page](https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/policies/registration-enrollment#incomplete-grades) for more details. We cannot expedite assigning you a grade, even if you're blocked from enrollment.

If you're not sure if taking an incomplete is right for you, we suggest reaching out to advising resources:
- [CS advising](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/resources/undergrads/cs/advising)
- [L&S advising](https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/appointments-advice)
- [CoE advising](https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/advising-counseling/ess-advising/)
- [CDSS advising](https://cdss.berkeley.edu/advising)
- [Summer Sessions advising](https://ssall.zendesk.com/hc/en-us)

If you are ineligible for an incomplete (e.g. have not finished enough assignments so far) but are still facing extenuating circumstances, or if you anticipate needing staff help while finishing the course, then late dropping the class and retaking it is another possible option. You can also withdraw from the summer semester with little or no consequences in the fall/spring semesters. Advising can answer any questions you have about late drops.

If you late drop the class due to extenuating circumstances, we will do our best to credit assignments you've completed toward future semesters, so that you don't have to redo assignments. We can't make any promises, though.


## Disclaimers

- If you were involved in a misconduct case and found responsible, we will revoke your incomplete.
- We will automatically change the "I" to whatever grade you have after your deadline passes, even if that grade is an F or NP.
- As per university policy from [L&S](https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/incomplete-grades), [CoE](https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/policies-procedures/grades/), and [CDSS](https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/policies/registration-enrollment#incomplete-grades), if you have 12+ units of incomplete grade at any instance of your academic career at Berkeley, you will not be allowed to enroll in classes next semester.
- It can take up to a month between the time you finish your last assignment/exam, and the time we resolve your grade. For example, if you take the final exam in August, you will not receive a grade in the class until September. We cannot expedite your grade assignment, even if you ask us. (This is because we need to prioritize assigning grades for students in the current semester before we can move on to resolving incompletes.)
